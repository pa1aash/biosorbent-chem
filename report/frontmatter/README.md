# report/frontmatter/ — Pages i-iv and the lists

## What belongs here

- `cover.tex` (official layout, Bible 14.1), `abstract.tex` (page i, one page maximum), `acknowledgement.tex` (page ii), `commitments.tex` (page iii, \includepdf of the scanned signed form), `toc.tex`.
- The Acknowledgement carries four mandatory declarations: AI usage, instrumentation/facility, prior-and-concurrent submission, and commercial interaction.

## What must never go here

- An implied declaration. Each of the four must be an explicit paragraph (Bible 2.4).
- A vague AI declaration. In 2026 that is a live disqualification vector, not a formality.
