# data/provided/column_A/ — Single-metal Pb fixed-bed column, three cycles

## What belongs here

- A bed-characterisation header per cycle: sorbent mass, measured bed height, V_bed, rho_b, H:D, EBCT, feed C0, pH, flow.
- Fraction-by-fraction effluent concentrations with cumulative volume AND cumulative bed volumes.
- The pooled regenerate concentration for each cycle.
- Any early termination (e.g. cycles stopped at C/C0 ~ 0.30) recorded explicitly as a data field, not omitted.

## What must never go here

- A breakthrough curve reduced to BV10 only. The fraction-level data is the evidence.
- Bed volumes computed from a nominal bed volume. BV must come from the MEASURED packed-bed volume.
