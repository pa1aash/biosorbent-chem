# data/provided/column_B/ — Ternary fixed-bed breakthrough

## What belongs here

- Fraction-by-fraction effluent concentrations for Pb, Cu AND Zn against cumulative bed volumes.
- The bed-characterisation header, as for Column A.
- The pooled regenerate analysed for all three metals.
- Any Cu/Zn overshoot (C/C0 > 1) recorded as measured — it is the signature of competitive displacement, not an error.

## What must never go here

- Interpolated points to smooth a curve.
- Lead-only analysis. All three metals are required for this run to mean anything.
