# data/provided/characterisation/loading/ — Galloyl loading quantification

## What belongs here

- Gravimetric tannin loading: dry mass after functionalisation and the equivalent unfunctionalised dry mass, per batch, n >= 3.
- Any orthogonal assay of loading actually performed (e.g. Folin-Ciocalteu on the residual liquor against a gallic-acid calibration), with its calibration data.

## What must never go here

- A loading figure quoted from the outline. The ~5.5 wt% claim is a claim to be tested, not an input (Attack #11).
