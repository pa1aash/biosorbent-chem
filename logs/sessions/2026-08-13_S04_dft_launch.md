## What the session was asked to do

Generate production ORCA input files from the locked protocol and launch them on the provisioned
Vultr box, working through eight phases in order and stopping at any phase that failed rather than
working around it. This was the critical path: DFT wall-clock is the one thing that cannot be
compressed later.

## Outcome

**All seventeen jobs launched at 21:12 IST on 13 August 2026.** No phase failed. Three faults that
would each have destroyed the run silently were found and dealt with before launch.

## Phase by phase

**Phase 0 — pre-flight.** SSH key confirmed passphrase-free; the box reachable on this network; the
working tree clean.

**Phase 1 — protocol extraction.** Every setting needed to write an input file was extracted from
`dft/DFT_PROTOCOL.md` alone into a table with its section reference. **No BLOCKING gaps.** Two items
were identified as derivations rather than transcriptions and recorded as such rather than presented
as quotations: that opt and freq run as one job (stated in §8 as a job *count* of 17, not as a
keyword directive), and that the quasi-RRHO cutoff is a post-processing step assigned to `thermo.py`
by `REACTIONS.md` §5.1 rather than an ORCA keyword.

**Phase 2 — job inventory.** Three-way cross-check of the species required by the nine exchange
equations (16), the protocol §8 job inventory (17), and the structures on disk (17). The single
difference is `pb_aquo8`, which is by design — its `.xyz` header carries `role=alternative` and no
reaction consumes it. S03 did not narrow the protonation states; all three remain in scope.
`pb_aquo8` was launched but ordered **last**, so it cannot delay a headline job.

**Phase 3 — input generation.** A generator, `dft/make_orca_inputs.py`, was written as the sole
writer of `dft/inputs/`. It transcribes every level-of-theory setting from the protocol and carries
the section reference into each `.inp`; it reads charge and multiplicity verbatim from the `.xyz`
provenance headers and **aborts rather than supplying a default** for a missing or self-contradictory
header. All seventeen files generated with no blanks. All four Cu(II) species carry explicit `UKS`
and multiplicity 2; all five Pb species declare the ECP explicitly.

**Phase 4 — remote pre-flight.** Bootstrap complete, OpenMPI **4.1.8** exactly, ORCA binary present
and executable, 16 vCPU, 30 GiB RAM, **431 GB free** — far beyond any plausible scratch requirement.
Beyond the listed checks, the production keyword line was **probed directly on the box before
uploading**. This is where the three faults below were found.

**Phase 5 — upload.** 17 directories, 17 `.inp`, **all seventeen md5 checksums identical** to local.
Smoke-test leftovers verified as genuinely smoke-test (a BP86/def2-SVP water optimisation,
unreferenced by any bootstrap script) and removed.

**Phase 6 — launch.** Queue launched in detached tmux session `dft-queue`. Verified running by tmux
session list, process list (8 MPI ranks per job, 2 jobs), `queue.log`, and output files being
written. `water` completed in 15 s with normal termination.

**Phases 7–8 — record and close-out.** `dft/JOB_QUEUE_STATUS.md`, `scripts/dft_status.sh`,
`scripts/dft_harvest.sh`, attack-register and decision-log updates.

## The three faults caught before launch

Each would have destroyed the run without an obvious cause.

| Fault | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **OpenMPI 4.1.8 refuses to run as root** without an explicit override. | **All seventeen jobs would have aborted in "Startup" within seconds**, logging failures that look like ORCA faults and are not. |
| **A tmux session inherits a non-login shell**, which on this box has no OpenMPI on `PATH` and an empty `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`. | Same outcome. |
| **`%maxcore 3000` overcommits memory 1.65×** — 3000 × 8 procs × 2 jobs = 48 GB against 29 GB available. | OOM kill at an arbitrary point, potentially hours into a job. |

The first two were fixed by explicit exports **inside `run_queue.sh`** — job-local environment
settings, not modifications to the box. The third was **raised with the author rather than silently
applied or silently changed**, and ruled down to 1500 MB before launch (**D-06**).

All three were found by direct probe on the box, not by reasoning about it.

  assumed column padding that does not exist).

include the SMD `G_CDS` term, or must `thermo.py` add it? Adding it twice would corrupt every free
energy in the report. The completed `water` job answers it from real output, with both arithmetic
identities closing exactly:

**`FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY` = E(SCF, SMD electrostatic) + G_CDS + E_D3BJ.** The CDS term is already
included; `thermo.py` must not add it again. Numbers in `dft/JOB_QUEUE_STATUS.md` §5. To be
re-confirmed on a metal complex before `thermo.py` is finalised.
