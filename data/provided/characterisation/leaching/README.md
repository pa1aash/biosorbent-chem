# data/provided/characterisation/leaching/ — Tannic-acid leaching test

## What belongs here

- Released phenolics from TA-OSS soaked in blank at pH 5 and at pH 2, quantified against a gallic-acid calibration curve, n >= 3.
- The gallic-acid calibration data itself.
- Soak time, liquid:solid ratio, temperature.

## What must never go here

- A qualitative 'no leaching observed' statement. Attack #10 asks whether the tannic acid is grafted or merely adsorbed; only a NUMBER answers it.
