# data/provided/characterisation/sem_edx/ — Electron microscopy and elemental mapping

## What belongs here

- SEM micrographs of RAW-OSS, fresh TA-OSS and Pb-loaded TA-OSS, with the scale bar burned in by the instrument.
- EDX elemental maps and point/area spectra, with the quantification table if the instrument produced one.
- Accelerating voltage, working distance, coating, magnification, operator and facility, per image.

## What must never go here

- Images with no scale bar.
- Images rescaled or cropped in a way that changes the stated magnification.
