# data/provided/characterisation/ash/ — Residual mineral (ash) content

## What belongs here

- Gravimetric records: crucible tare, dry mass, ash mass, for RAW-OSS and TA-OSS, in duplicate.
- Furnace temperature and hold time.

## What must never go here

- Percentages without the underlying masses. The percentage is derived.
