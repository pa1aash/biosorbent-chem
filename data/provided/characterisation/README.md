# data/provided/characterisation/ — Material characterisation evidence

## What belongs here

- Sub-directories by technique: `ftir/`, `xps/`, `sem_edx/`, `ash/`, `ph_pzc/`, `loading/`, `leaching/`.
- Instrumentally acquired evidence that the material is what the report says it is.

## What must never go here

- TGA or BET data. Neither instrument was available; both are formally DESIGNED OUT in `docs/02_PROTOCOL_AUDIT.md` item B5. Do not create placeholders that imply they exist.
