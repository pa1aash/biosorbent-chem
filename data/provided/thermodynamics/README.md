# data/provided/thermodynamics/ — Temperature series for the van 't Hoff analysis

## What belongs here

- Equilibrium uptake at >= 3 controlled temperatures, with the temperature recorded as measured, not as a setpoint.
- The concentration series at each temperature needed to obtain the equilibrium constant.
- Replicate structure recorded per point.

## What must never go here

- Data collected at 'ambient'. This analysis requires controlled, measured temperatures.
- Derived Delta-H, Delta-S or Delta-G values — those come from `analysis/src/thermo.py`.
