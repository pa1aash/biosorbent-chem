# refs/pdfs/ — PDFs of cited works

## What belongs here

- One PDF per entry in `library.bib`, named by citation key.
- Their presence is the evidence that the 'never cite a paper you have not read' rule was kept — referees ask what reference 23 found.

## What must never go here

- Papers that are cited but not held. If the PDF cannot be obtained, the abstract and figures must at minimum have been read, and that is recorded in `VERIFICATION_LOG.csv`.
