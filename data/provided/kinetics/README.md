# data/provided/kinetics/ — Time-course uptake data

## What belongs here

- Pb and Cu kinetic series: contact time vs residual concentration, including the 60 min duplicate.
- Sorbent mass and solution volume per flask, so q_t is recomputable from first principles.

## What must never go here

- Fitted rate constants. Non-linear fits are produced by `analysis/src/kinetics.py` into `data/processed/`.
- Linearised transforms of any kind.
