# admin/submission/ — The artefacts actually sent to the Organising Committee

## What belongs here

- `Chem-151-Research Report.pdf` — the final single PDF. The filename must be exactly this.
- The zipped appendix bundle, if appendices are supplied separately.
- If the bundle exceeds 5 MB: the download link and a record of where it is hosted. Do NOT email >5 MB.
- A copy of the sent email.

## What must never go here

- Draft builds. Only the artefact that was actually submitted, or is about to be.
- More than one PDF of the report body. The rules require ONE single file.
