# admin/signatures/ — Scanned signed documents

## What belongs here

- The signed Commitments form, scanned at >= 300 dpi, flat, not photographed at an angle.
- Required signatures: Palaash Gang (team member), 'Nil' on unused member lines, Yogita Hastak Menon (supervising teacher), and the School Principal of Indus International School, Pune ('Noted and endorsed by').
- Filename convention: `Commitments_signed_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`.

## What must never go here

- Unsigned drafts.
- Low-resolution phone photographs. A skewed or <300 dpi scan is a visible-quality failure on page iii of the submitted report.
