# dft/validation/ — Protocol validation against known quantities

## What belongs here

- The validation job: a known Pb-O bond length or hydration-free-energy trend reproduced under the exact production protocol, with the deviation reported.
- This is short, cheap and disproportionately credibility-generating (Bible 3.6).

## What must never go here

- Validation run at a different level of theory from production. It then validates nothing.
