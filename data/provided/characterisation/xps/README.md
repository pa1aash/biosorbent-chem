# data/provided/characterisation/xps/ — X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy

## What belongs here

- Survey scans and high-resolution regions as binding-energy / intensity CSVs.
- Required regions: Pb 4f, O 1s, C 1s, N 1s, on RAW-OSS, fresh TA-OSS and Pb-loaded TA-OSS.
- The charge-reference used (adventitious C 1s at 284.8 eV) recorded per file.
- Instrument, source, pass energy, step size, operator and facility.

## What must never go here

- Vendor-software peak-fit output presented as raw data. Fits are reproduced in `analysis/`.
- Spectra with no stated charge reference — binding energies are meaningless without one.
