# data/provided/batch/ — Batch equilibrium data

## What belongs here

- pH optimisation series (TA-OSS and RAW-OSS, plus the sorbent-free precipitation control).
- Single-metal isotherms: Pb, Cu, Zn on TA-OSS, plus the RAW-OSS comparator.
- Measured initial and equilibrium concentrations with dilution factors, per flask, per replicate.

## What must never go here

- Fitted Langmuir/Freundlich/Sips parameters — those are computed into `data/processed/`.
- Mean-only summaries. Every replicate is a row.
